About NOVA Journal

NOVA Journal is a technology and creativity blog created by Yongama Goso, an Information Systems student, emerging software developer, and technology entrepreneur from South Africa.

The blog explores the ideas, tools, and stories shaping technology, business, education, and digital creativity. It focuses on practical, accessible writing about artificial intelligence, software development, entrepreneurship, innovation, skills development, and the changing digital world.

It was created as a space to document learning, share useful insights, discuss new ideas, and show practical projects — combining technology with creativity to make complex topics easier to understand for students, entrepreneurs, developers, and curious learners.

The name NOVA refers to a sudden burst of light: a bright new beginning. The journal aims to highlight new ideas and encourage people to explore what they can build in the digital age.

Mission and vision

Mission

To make technology, innovation, and entrepreneurship easier to understand and more accessible to students, creators, developers, and emerging business builders — encouraging practical learning, creative thinking, and responsible use of technology to solve real-world problems.

Vision

To become a trusted digital space where ideas, technology, and creativity come together to inspire learning, innovation, and meaningful opportunities.

How the website was built

NOVA Journal is a front-end website built with HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript. It uses localStorage to store blog posts, bookmarks, comments, newsletter sign-ups, and contact messages in the browser. The FileReader API handles featured-image uploads, which are resized on a <canvas> and stored as Base64 data — so images work without a backend or database.

The project demonstrates responsive design, page navigation, form validation, image handling, search, category filtering, sorting, dark mode, dynamic content rendering, and accessible interface design.

Key features

  • Dynamic featured editorial and latest-articles feed on the home page
  • Search, topic filtering, sorting, saved bookmarks, and load-more pagination
  • A full create-post editor with live preview, validation, and image compression
  • Single-article view with breadcrumbs, reading progress, sharing, and comments
  • Dark and light themes with the preference remembered between visits
  • Graceful behaviour when storage is full, blocked, or an image fails to load
